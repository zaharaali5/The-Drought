About Me

The Drought makes alternative rock in Salt Lake City. We are equally invested in lyricism and arrangement, blending folk, rock, indie, and jazz influences.

The project explores erosion, fragile systems, economic anxiety, and consuming love. It is impossible to escape obsession amidst the absurdism of modernity.
